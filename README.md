## `01 / active systems`

### [`DARIA`](https://github.com/dariumi/Daria)

**Desktop AI Reactive Intelligent Assistant**

A local AI companion with long-term memory, voice, plugins, RAG and a desktop-like web interface.

The idea is simple: AI should feel like something that **lives on your machine**, not another tab with a chat box.

`Python` · `Ollama` · `Local LLM` · `RAG` · `Voice` · `Memory`

---

### [`veil`](https://github.com/dariumi/veil)

**privacy infrastructure**

A self-hosted network tunnel built around QUIC, TLS 1.3 and HTTP/3 camouflage, with VPN and proxy modes.

`Rust` · `QUIC` · `TLS 1.3` · `TUN` · `SOCKS5` · `Tauri`

---

### [`Daphne`](https://github.com/dariumi/Daphne)

**data layer**

Python tooling for loading, cleaning, preparing, evaluating and managing datasets.

A small attempt to make the repetitive parts of data work less repetitive.

`Python` · `pandas` · `scikit-learn` · `data tooling`

---

### [`Arkonova`](https://github.com/dariumi/Arkonova-Network)

**umbrella / experiments**

A place for infrastructure, ML work and projects that do not fit neatly into one box.
